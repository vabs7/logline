Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Cinerolls is built with minimal data collection principles in mind. We collect only what is strictly necessary to authenticate users, prevent spam, and maintain platform integrity:

  • Email Address: Collected strictly through Firebase Authentication for email link passwordless sign-in. Your email proves control over your account.
  • Display Handle: An optional 3–20 character pseudonym you may set in your dashboard for display purposes.
  • Timestamps: Account creation timestamp, logline submission and revision timestamps, rating timestamps, and a lastSubmitAt timestamp used strictly to enforce the 10-minute submission throttle.

2. What We Do NOT Collect or Do

  • No Full Scripts: We store loglines only, never full scripts, screenplays, or treatments.
  • No Advertisements: Cinerolls does not serve commercial third-party ads.
  • No Third-Party Trackers: We do not embed marketing trackers, tracking pixels, behavioral telemetry, or social network surveillance scripts.
  • No Sale of Data: We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or contact details.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored directly in Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore databases located in secure data centers. Client writes and reads are strictly governed by granular Firestore security rules enforcing user ownership, author privileges, rating formulas, and administrative permissions without requiring server-side compute.

4. Contact & Deletion

If you wish to delete your account or withdraw submissions, you may do so directly through your account dashboard or by contacting the site administrator.
